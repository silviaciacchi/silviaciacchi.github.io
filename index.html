About

I am a researcher, consultant and lecturer specialising in Business and Human Rights, EU law, and corporate sustainability due diligence.

I hold a PhD in Law and Economics from Erasmus University Rotterdam. My work combines legal and interdisciplinary research with applied projects, focusing on the regulation of global value chains, corporate accountability, and the practical implementation of sustainability and human rights due diligence.
