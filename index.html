Silvia Ciacchi

Researcher · Consultant · Lecturer

Business & Human Rights · EU Law · Sustainability Due Diligence

Silvia Ciacchi is a researcher, consultant and lecturer specialising in Business and Human Rights, EU law, and corporate sustainability due diligence.

She holds a PhD in Law and Economics from Erasmus University Rotterdam. Her work combines legal and interdisciplinary research with applied projects, focusing on the regulation of global value chains, corporate accountability, and the practical implementation of human rights and sustainability due diligence.

Her professional activities connect academic research with policy development and business practice through consulting, teaching, training and collaborative initiatives.
